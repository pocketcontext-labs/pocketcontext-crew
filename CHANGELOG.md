One line per PocketContext Crew release, newest first. Each version links its
release notes in [docs/releases/](docs/releases/); upgrade steps are in
[docs/upgrades.md](docs/upgrades.md). Downloads are on the
[GitHub releases page](https://github.com/pocketcontext-labs/pocketcontext-crew/releases).
