# Crew 0.32.1

One reader fix. No data migration, protocol, managed block, skill or capabilities
change.

- **Refresh answers a tap.** On an iPhone, tapping the ↻ Refresh button at the top
  right showed nothing: no pressed state, no sign of loading and no confirmation, so
  you could not tell whether it worked. Now the button (and the phone Search icon)
  darkens and shrinks slightly while pressed, including in Safari and the
  home-screen app; while the workspace reloads the ↻ icon spins and the button is
  disabled, so a second tap does not start another load; and when it ends a small
  "Updated." appears under the header buttons for a moment, or "Couldn't refresh.
  Check your connection and try again." when it fails. With reduced motion the
  icon does not spin; the button stays tinted while loading instead. The silent
  background refresh every 30 seconds is unchanged.

## Upgrade

Replace the binary or image and upgrade paired clients to 0.32.1; see
[Crew 0.32.0 to 0.32.1](../upgrades.md). From 0.31.0 or earlier, follow the
0.31.0 to 0.32.0 notes first.
