# Crew 0.32.0

Reader only. No data migration, protocol, managed block, skill or capabilities
change.

- **Pick any options, with feedback on each.** A review with options shows them as
  cards with checkboxes under "Options · pick any" (or "Text · pick any" and
  "Images · pick any" when some options are a single image, shown as tiles). Each
  option has a feedback icon: checking an option, or tapping the icon (also on an
  option you leave unchecked), opens a one-line "Feedback on …" field under it, and
  an image tile with feedback shows a badge. The button counts your picks,
  "Approve 3 selected", and Approve needs at least one. Approve and Request changes
  record every option in `option_decisions`: checked as `approved`, unchecked as
  `rejected`, each with its feedback as `note`, so the requesting agent reads exactly
  what you picked and what to change. Reject rejects every option and keeps the
  feedback. A decided review shows the same picks and feedback.
- **A compact decision footer.** The footer under a pending review is a one-line
  "Add a note…" field (it grows upward while you type) and one row with
  "Approve N selected" (or "Approve draft") beside Request changes, at most a quarter
  of a phone screen, so the draft and its options get the rest. Secondary actions are
  in the header's More menu: Add note (focuses the note), Copy link, Share where the
  browser can share, and Reject, last and in red. The buttons never move: not when
  the note opens, not when More opens or closes, not from one review to the next.
- **Previous and next instead of swiping.** Swiping between items is gone. A task,
  note, bucket, agent or review opened from a list has a capsule after the title,
  `∧ 3 of 12 ∨`, on phones too: vertical chevrons for the list (J and K on a
  keyboard), so they never look like the horizontal back and forward buttons on the
  left. The ends are disabled in place and nothing wraps; a dialog with no list
  behind it hides the capsule. Each step pushes one history entry, so the browser's
  Back and Forward, iOS edge-swipe back and the dialog's own back and forward retrace
  the items you moved through; back from the first item returns to the list. Closing
  returns to the list's own history entry with its scroll and filters, so Back does
  not reopen the item. Buckets and agents now open with their link in the address,
  like tasks and notes. Copy link and Share as artifact moved from the header into
  More, so every header is one row at 320 px.

## Upgrade

Replace the binary or image and upgrade paired clients to 0.32.0; see
[Crew 0.31.0 to 0.32.0](../upgrades.md). From 0.29.0 or earlier, follow the
0.29.0 to 0.30.0 notes first.
