# Crew 0.37.1

Three reader fixes. No data migration, protocol, managed block, skill or
capabilities change.

- **Copy a review draft as plain text.** The draft box in a Review draft has a
  Copy button in its top right corner, and every text option of a pick-any review
  has a copy icon beside its feedback icon. Copy puts the exact snapshot on the
  clipboard as plain text, ready to paste into WhatsApp or another app: Markdown
  symbols are removed, paragraphs and line breaks are kept, list items become
  "•" (numbered items keep their numbers), external links keep their address in
  brackets. The button reads "Copied" (or "Not copied") for 2 seconds without
  changing size or moving. It works on pending and decided reviews and in a
  task's Reviews tab; the ⋯ menu also has Copy text.
- **An option's feedback icon focuses its field.** Tapping the feedback icon on
  a review option now opens that option's feedback field and focuses it in the
  same tap, so the iPhone keyboard opens, and scrolls it into view above the
  footer. The note stays tied to that option (`option_decisions` note); tapping
  another option's icon moves to that option's own field. Before, the icon looked
  for a one-line input that had become a growing text field in 0.33.0, so it never
  focused anything, and on a checked option it closed the field. The icons are
  44 px tap targets now, and the card head keeps its height.
- **Shared filters by one status at a time.** The Shared page replaces the four
  toggle chips, which wrapped to two rows on a phone and showed no clear selection,
  with the switcher Tasks and Notes use, on every width: `‹  Live · 3  (2 of 4)  ›`
  over Drafts, Live, Revoked (revoked or expired) and Archived. The arrows never
  move and are greyed out in place at the ends; tapping the name opens a picker
  with live counts and a check on the current status. Without a status in the
  address it opens on Live, or on the first status that has shared pages when Live
  is empty; a focused shared page opens on its own status. The status stays in the
  address (`#/shared?show=live`), and older addresses with several statuses open on
  the first of them. Nothing looks selected from hover on touch screens.

## Upgrade

Replace the binary or image and upgrade paired clients to 0.37.1; see
[Crew 0.37.0 to 0.37.1](../upgrades.md). From 0.36.0 or earlier, follow the
0.36.0 to 0.37.0 notes first.
