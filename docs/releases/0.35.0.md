# Crew 0.35.0

Previous/next on every detail page, one phone switcher for Tasks and Notes, and the
Crew mark in the header. No data migration, protocol, managed block, skill or
capabilities change.

- **Previous/next everywhere.** Every record dialog (task, note, bucket, agent,
  approval) shows the `∧ x of y ∨` cluster wherever you opened it. An item opened
  from a Home section steps through that section only: approvals waiting, the
  Needs you tasks, Recent (tasks and notes together) or Agents, in the order Home
  shows them, and the section is kept in the browser history, so Back, Forward and
  a reload keep the same list. A record opened from a link steps through its own
  collection's list (the board, or its column on a phone; all notes; buckets;
  agents; pending or past approvals). When a filter hides the open item, the
  controls are hidden but keep their place, so ⋯ and ✕ never move. History ‹ ›
  stays separate, J/K still step, the ends are disabled in place and nothing swipes
  between items. An approval opened from Home now has its own
  `#/approvals/<id>` link, and closing returns to Home.
- **Shared pages step too.** On Shared, an artifact's title opens it focused
  (`#/shared/<id>`), and the focused card has the same `∧ x of y ∨` (and J/K)
  through the Shared list as filtered.
- **One phone switcher.** The Notes bucket switcher and the Tasks column switcher
  are the same component and look identical: a fixed-height row, a plain centred
  name with its live count and "(x of y)", the same 44 px arrows (disabled in place
  at the ends) and the same hairline divider. The tinted pill on Notes and the dots
  under the Tasks switcher are gone. Tapping the name still opens the picker.
- **The Crew mark in the header.** The top-left logo in the phone top bar, the
  desktop sidebar, sign-in and upload pages is the Crew app mark (the same artwork
  as the home-screen icon and favicon) instead of the PocketContext shield, at the
  same size and named "Crew".

## Upgrade

Replace the binary or image and upgrade paired clients to 0.35.0; see
[Crew 0.34.0 to 0.35.0](../upgrades.md). From 0.33.1 or earlier, follow the earlier
notes first.
