# Crew 0.14.0

Reader improvements. No data format, protocol version, skill, capabilities document,
managed instruction block or public route changed, so `capabilities_digest` is unchanged.
Saved webhook settings are not changed by the upgrade.

## Tasks and Notes

- The AI Team page is now **Tasks** (`#/tasks`) and the Shared memory page is now
  **Notes** (`#/notes`). Old `#/team…` and `#/memory…` addresses redirect and keep their
  filters, and permanent item links keep working.
- A task or note link opened directly shows the item over its own list. Items opened from
  inside the reader still open over the page you were on.
- The MCP tools, skill and documentation still use the term "shared memory"; that wider
  rename is planned separately.

## Home

- Each "Your crew right now" tile opens the Tasks board focused on its column: the board
  scrolls to the column, moves focus to it and briefly highlights it. All columns and
  filters stay as they were. With reduced motion the scroll is instant and the highlight
  is static.

## Wake rules

- The webhook wake rules and the bot handoff limit moved out of Advanced settings into a
  visible **Wake rules** section with one Save button.
- Each rule explains when it wakes a bot and what turning it off means, and shows its
  recommended value: on for Created ready, Assigned, Reassigned, Marked ready, Reopened,
  Review resolved and Explicit catch-up; off for Unassigned (triage) and Owner comment.
  The recommended handoff limit is 4. These match the defaults of a new install.
- Reset to recommended fills the form without saving. Saving sends only what changed and,
  because changed rules turn delivery off and cancel queued wakes, asks for confirmation
  first. Advanced settings keeps the role and reporting instructions.

## Connect

- The Connect page now says correctly that connected apps can decide a review when you
  ask them to in the conversation (possible since 0.12.0).

## Upgrade

Replace the 0.13.0 binary or image with 0.14.0 following the
[upgrade procedure](../upgrades.md), and upgrade paired clients with the server.
