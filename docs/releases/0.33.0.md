# Crew 0.33.0

A phone-first reader: a slim top bar with bottom tabs, a one-column Tasks board
with a column switcher, Notes that start on screen, relative dates everywhere,
fields that wrap, a compact claim banner and a way back from shared pages in the
home-screen app. No data migration, protocol, managed block, skill or capabilities
change.

- **Compact phone chrome.** On phones the seven-item menu grid is gone. A slim
  one-row top bar holds the Crew mark, the page title, search, the page's filter
  icon (with a count when filters are on), + (New task, New note, New agent) and ⋯.
  The ⋯ sheet holds the page intro, help, Refresh and page actions such as New
  bucket. Bottom tabs: Home, Tasks, Notes, Approvals (with the pending count) and
  More, which opens Shared, Agents, Archive, Connect an agent, Guide and Sign out.
  The tabs leave room for the iPhone home bar. Desktop is unchanged.
- **Tasks board on phones.** Cards start right under a sticky column switcher,
  `‹ To do · 6 (1 of 4) ›` with dots: previous and next stay in place and are
  disabled at the ends, and tapping the column name opens a picker with every
  column and its live count (no swipe). Search, scope, sort, agents and the other
  filters are in a Filters sheet with Done; active filters show as removable chips
  under the top bar. A task opened from the board steps through its column.
- **Notes on phones.** A sticky row of bucket chips with counts ("All · 396",
  then each bucket) sits under the top bar, and the notes list starts right below
  it: title, bucket, relative date and a one-line snippet, with record ids in the
  snippet shown as their titles. The selected bucket chip's ⋯ edits the bucket; a
  filter and sort sheet has search, sort, buckets (each with ⋯ Edit) and the
  responsible agent.
- **Relative dates everywhere.** One shared formatter: "just now", "5 min ago",
  "19 h ago", "yesterday", "3 days ago" up to 7 days, then a short date ("Nov 10",
  with the year when it differs), in your locale and timezone. Hover or long-press
  shows the exact local time, the markup carries `<time datetime>`, and visible
  dates refresh while the page is open. Activity reads `Updated · 19 h ago · Assess`.
  This covers activity, comments, cards, notes, approvals, Home, Archive, Shared,
  Connect, file settings, upload links, webhook status and session instructions.
- **Fields that wrap.** Title, Hold reason and the other one-line text fields in
  dialogs wrap and grow to about four lines before scrolling inside, never
  sideways. Enter still saves, pasted line breaks become spaces, the text is 16 px
  on phones (so iOS doesn't zoom) and AutoFill is off (no "AutoFill Contact").
- **Linked hold reasons.** Hold reason has a Read and Edit view: record ids and
  Crew addresses read as the record's title and open it as a step of the link
  trail; tap the text or ✎ Edit to change the raw text. Ids in Activity, decision
  notes and revision reasons are links too.
- **Compact claim banner.** Edit task shows a claim in two lines: "Claimed ·
  Implement" or "Claim expired · Implement" with a ⋯ menu (Copy details, Release
  claim), then "Started 15 min ago · ends in 1 h 45 m" (or "expired 13 h ago") and
  a Details control for the tool and session. Form groups are evenly spaced and
  the Labels box is only as tall as its chips. Card claim chips read "Codex · 1 h
  40 m left".
- **A way back from shared pages in the home-screen app.** In the iPhone
  home-screen app, Open on a live shared page (and a link to one in a note or task)
  shows it in an in-app preview with ‹ Back to Shared, the title and a ⋯ menu (Copy
  link, Open in Safari, Share); Shared keeps its filter and scroll position. In a
  browser, Open still opens a new tab. The public page may now be framed by Crew's
  own origin only (`frame-ancestors 'self'`, `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`), opens
  external links in a new tab, and, if it is ever opened in the home-screen app's
  own window, shows a CSS-only "Back to Crew" bar there (never in a browser). It
  still has no script, and previews are not counted as views.

## Upgrade

Replace the binary or image and upgrade paired clients to 0.33.0; see
[Crew 0.32.1 to 0.33.0](../upgrades.md). From 0.31.0 or earlier, follow the
0.31.0 to 0.32.0 notes first.
